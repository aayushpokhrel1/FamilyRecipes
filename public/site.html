What it does for a kitchen

What it does

  • A private vault Every recipe is private, family-only or public, one recipe at a time. Nothing is public because of a default.
  • The story, not just the method Who it came from and who changed it, kept with the ingredients and the steps, because that is the part that gets lost.
  • Put one in from a photo or your voice Photograph a handwritten card, paste a page, or just talk. It drafts the recipe and you check it before anything is saved.
  • For the day you cook it A grocery list that knows an either-or ingredient is one item, and a cooking view in big type that keeps the screen awake.
